# MITRE Mapping Plan

## Stage Vocabulary

The initial product vocabulary is: Reconnaissance, Initial Access, Lateral Movement, Command and Control, and Exfiltration. Execution, Persistence, Credential Access, and other techniques may be shown when supported by evidence.

## Mapping Approach

Mappings combine documented dataset labels, observable feature patterns, and model stage output. Every mapping has a confidence level and provenance.

## Rules

- Do not force a stage when evidence is insufficient.
- Do not imply a guaranteed linear kill chain.
- Distinguish observed technique evidence from predicted stage.
- Keep the mapping versioned and reviewable.

## Example

Repeated new destinations may support a reconnaissance hypothesis. Authentication anomalies followed by new internal connections may support a lateral-movement forecast. These are evidence associations, not universal rules.
